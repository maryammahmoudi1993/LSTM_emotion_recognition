# Credits

All figures in this repository are drawn by the code in this repository (matplotlib).
They show statistics of the **IMDB movie review data set** (Maas et al., 2011, <https://aclanthology.org/P11-1015/>),
loaded through Keras; apart from the sentences written for teaching, the only review text is a short excerpt (about 400 characters) of one training review, shown in the output of notebook 01.

Notebook 02 also uses the GloVe vectors (Pennington, Socher and Manning, 2014, <https://aclanthology.org/D14-1162/>,
distributed by gensim-data under the licence listed there, PDDL) and the VADER tool (Hutto and Gilbert, 2014, MIT licence); see [`../data/README.md`](../data/README.md).

No images from the internet are used.
