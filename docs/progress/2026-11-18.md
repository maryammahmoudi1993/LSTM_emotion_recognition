## Results (all from the executed notebook, seed 42, CPU; 20,000 training / 5,000 validation reviews, at most 6 epochs, best-validation-epoch weights kept)

| Model | Validation accuracy (best epoch) | Test accuracy (25,000 reviews) |
|---|---|---|
| Bag of words + logistic regression | 0.8796 | 0.8572 |
| SimpleRNN | 0.786 | 0.7966 |
| LSTM | 0.872 | 0.8626 |

- The LSTM and the word-counting baseline are about half a point apart on the test set: no claim that one is better.
- The SimpleRNN is unstable (validation accuracy between 0.54 and 0.79 over the epochs).
- Running the notebook twice gave identical numbers.
