## What was blocked, exactly

The planned comparison with a **small pretrained language model (Transformer)** could not be done:
`huggingface.co` (the host that serves such models) refused the connection from this environment
(the proxy answered "CONNECT tunnel failed, response 403"). Nothing was mocked or invented. What was reachable and is used instead:

- **GloVe pretrained word vectors** (gensim-data release on GitHub, checksum pinned) to start an LSTM from pretrained knowledge (frozen and fine-tuned);
- **VADER**, a ready-made rule-based sentiment tool (PyPI, MIT licence).

The notebook says clearly that these are not pretrained language models and that no Transformer result exists.
If `huggingface.co` (and its download hosts) were added to the allowed network domains of the environment, the natural next experiment would be a ready-made sentiment model on the same reviews.

## Results (from the executed notebook, CPU; 20,000 training / 5,000 validation reviews, 25,000 test reviews)

| Model | Test accuracy |
|---|---|
| LSTM + GloVe fine-tuned (seeds 42, 1, 2) | 0.8704 mean (0.8661 to 0.8738) |
| LSTM from scratch (seeds 42, 1, 2) | 0.8625 mean (0.8574 to 0.8675) |
| Bag of words + logistic regression | 0.8572 |
| LSTM + GloVe frozen (seed 42) | 0.8285 |
| VADER (rebuilt text without punctuation) | 0.6984 |

Fine-tuned GloVe was ahead for every seed (+0.35, +0.63, +1.40 points): a small, consistent gain, not a proof. Running the notebook twice gave identical numbers.

## Checks

## Doubts / open points

- No Transformer result (blocked host, see above).
- The GloVe model is slightly larger (50 instead of 32 coordinates per word) than the from-scratch model; the gain may be partly due to size.
- The frozen variant ran for six epochs only and was still improving.
- GloVe licence (PDDL) taken from the gensim-data list; the original Stanford page was not reachable. The IMDB data licence is still unverified (see `data/README.md`).
- The notebook now takes about twelve minutes of CPU time (the whole test suite about seventeen).
